📋 CA Day in the Life & Reporting Off — Reference for Bedside Shift Report competency
▸ Show
How a full day should possibly look — pending on rapids, codes, BERTs, and patient flow.
6:30–7:15Report (bedside shift report), huddle
7:15–8:00VS, BS, get patient up in chair (if applicable) for breakfast
8:00–9:00Breakfast
9:00–12:00VS (if applicable), 1st walk of the day, hygiene, BS for lunch, turns/repositions/incontinence care
12:00–13:00Lunch
13:00–14:302nd walk of the day, turns/repositions/incontinence care, empty drains/foleys/suction canisters, duties, linens, cleaning fridge, dirty utility, etc. Prep for evenings
14:30–15:00Report (bedside shift report)
15:00–17:00VS, BS for dinner, 3rd walk of the day (maybe up in chair for dinner)
17:00–18:00Dinner
19:00–22:00Up walking for the 4th time of day, nighttime hygiene, bedtime BS, turns/repositions/incontinence care, empty foleys/drains/suction canisters, duties, linens, cleaning fridge, dirty utility, etc. Prep for evenings
22:30–23:00Report (bedside shift report)
23:00–00:30Vitals, BS if applicable, turns/repositions/incontinence care
00:30–03:00Duties, linens, cleaning fridge, dirty utility, etc.
03:00–05:00VS, turns/repositions/incontinence care, blood work
05:00–06:30Empty foleys/drains/suction canisters, BS if applicable, prep unit for dayshift
How reporting off should look:
  • Room #, name, code status
  • How they ambulate (AM-PAC score) or if complete care patient (turns & incontinence care)
  • How they void and have BMs (attends, purewick, foley, ostomy, urinal, commode, bathroom)
  • Any drains that need emptying
  • Diet (feed assist or tray setup)
  • VS (how often & when last taken)
  • BS checks (how often)
  • If they have a specialty line (PICC/Medi port) — arms that cannot have BP or venipuncture from
  • Any blood work hanging out or soon to come
  • Precautions: Braden & Morse fall scale score; skin or fall precautions needed
  • Bracelet, fall sign & lights, alarm, bed low, bed cords plugged in, gait belt, nonskid footwear
  • Mepilex, boots or pillows for heels, waffle cushion, wedges, specialty bed
  • Discuss plan of care if known with oncoming shift (test, surgery, discharge possibly)
