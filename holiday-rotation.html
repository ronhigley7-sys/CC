Holiday assignments

Exactly one winter and one summer holiday per year unless staffing requires more. Volunteers first. Christmas Eve/Day alternate based on verified prior-year assignments. Keep regular shifts. Agency contracts covering part of a season require one eligible holiday if one remains. Assignments are proposals until approved.
